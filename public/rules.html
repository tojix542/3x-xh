Rules of Use

By accessing the 3x Forensics platform, you agree to abide by the following terms. Violations result in immediate account suspension and ban.

1 Account & Authentication
  • 🔐
    Discord Authentication Required

    All users must authenticate via Discord OAuth2. Access is gated by Customer role membership in the controller guild. No exceptions.

    Required
  • 🚫
    No Account Sharing

    Your Discord account is tied to your 3x session. Sharing credentials, tokens, or session data with others is prohibited and results in permanent ban.

    Instant Ban
2 Scan Conduct
  • 🖥️
    No Self-Scanning

    Customers are strictly prohibited from scanning their own machine or account. The 3x Agent collects Discord tokens, HWID, and user ID data. Self-scanning is detected and results in immediate account termination.

    Instant Ban
  • ⏱️
    24-Hour Cooldown — Maximum 2 Scans

    Each customer is limited to 2 scans per 24-hour period. Attempting to exceed this limit will trigger an automatic ban. The cooldown is enforced server-side and cannot be bypassed.

    2 Scans / 24h
  • 🎯
    Authorized Targets Only

    Scans may only be performed on targets you have explicit authorization to investigate. Unauthorized scanning of third parties is prohibited.

    Violation = Ban
3 Data Collection
  • 📊
    Forensic Data Logging

    All scan sessions log: Discord user ID, Discord token (truncated), HWID, scan results, risk score, and timestamp. This data is stored securely and accessible only to the platform owner.

    Logged
  • 🔒
    Data Retention

    Scan logs are retained indefinitely for security and audit purposes. Customers may request log review through the owner. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

    Indefinite
4 Enforcement
  • ⚡
    Automatic Enforcement

    The system automatically detects and enforces: self-scan attempts, cooldown violations, role removal, and suspicious activity patterns. Bans are applied instantly without warning.

    Auto-Ban
  • 🛡️
    Appeals

    Ban appeals may be submitted to the platform owner via Discord. Appeals are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. False positives are rare but possible.

    Owner Review
Last Updated: July 27, 2026
These rules are subject to change without notice. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the current terms. For questions, contact the platform owner via Discord.
